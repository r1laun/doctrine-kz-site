We invite physicians to a conference dedicated to modern approaches to the diagnosis, treatment and long-term follow-up of patients with cardiovascular diseases and comorbidity. In everyday practice, rhythm disorders, arterial hypertension, coronary artery disease and heart failure often occur together with conditions of other organs and systems — the program helps to account for this interaction when examining patients and choosing therapy.

Key topics

  • Arrhythmology: diagnosis and treatment of rhythm disorders, specifics of managing patients in selected groups
  • Implantable devices: what a cardiologist, a therapist and a general practitioner need to know
  • Dyslipidaemia and atherosclerosis
  • Coronary artery disease: diagnosis, risk assessment, treatment
  • Chronic heart failure and rhythm disorders
  • Arterial hypertension and its link to arrhythmias
  • Functional diagnostics: ECG, Holter monitoring, ABPM, EchoCG, strain analysis, stress tests
  • The comorbid patient: cardiovascular disease combined with neurological, psychiatric, pulmonological and gastroenterological pathology
  • Rational pharmacotherapy and the place of nutraceuticals in comprehensive patient management
Special focus

Special attention will be paid to anxiety disorders, panic attacks, PTSD, dementia and parkinsonism in the work of a physician managing a cardiac patient.

What to expect

Specialist talks and clinical case discussions with practical algorithms, a medical exhibition, networking with colleagues and partner companies, and a contest for physicians with certificates for training at the Doctrine centre and other prizes. There will be an online broadcast; recordings of the talks will become available later.

Who it's for

Cardiologists, arrhythmologists, therapists, general practitioners, functional diagnostics specialists, neurologists, pulmonologists, gastroenterologists, endocrinologists, clinical pharmacologists and other specialists managing patients with cardiovascular and combined conditions.
