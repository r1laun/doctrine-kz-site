FAQ

Yes. You receive a centre certificate; selected webinars grant credit units. See each course card for details.

How do I enroll?

Click “Enroll” - a participant form (Google Form) opens. Fill it in and we will contact you on WhatsApp.

How does online work?

Live webinars + recordings. Links and materials arrive by email and WhatsApp.

In Almaty. Ask the coordinator on WhatsApp for the exact venue.

Can I pay in installments?

Message us on WhatsApp - we will find a suitable option.
